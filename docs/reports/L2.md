# L2 report: MotionBricks SOMA-30 to Unity Humanoid and SA humanoid-v1

## 1 Verdict: YELLOW
Mapping is feasible: all 15 Unity-required slots and all 15 SA requiredBones have a SOMA source, and 25 of 30 joints map one-to-one. Costs: (a) rest pose is an A-pose (~39 deg upper-arm abduction) while Unity and SA want T-pose, so per-bone world-space rest correction is needed; (b) handedness mirror (SOMA left = +X, SA/Unity left = -X); (c) local bone axes are not axis-aligned (X runs along the bone), so local Euler or quaternion copying is invalid; (d) Neck2 and 4 fingertip markers have no target; (e) NVIDIA sample BVHs are evaluation-only licence.

## 4 Conflicts
- Up axis: earlier scan versus real file; resolved as Y-up.
- Handedness: SA file says right = +X, forward +Z, up +Y (left-handed, Unity-style). SOMA is right-handed with left = +X. Needs an X mirror: p' = (-x, y, z), q' = (qx, -qy, -qz, qw), applied on world-space data, then converted back to locals.
- "T-pose parameterization" holds inside the ONNX wrapper only; the interface is A-pose.
