## Diagnosis

- **Frame 60 of the full take is already on the ground.** The lowest sole is 0.2 cm on Synty, and touchdown is at frame 1
  in both the source and the output. So this J1 tests a landing-to-idle bridge, not a mid-air one. A mid-air anchor
  (take frames 30-45) is needed to exercise the arc.
- **G4: the generator brakes at once toward the standing goal.** The context carries the velocity: the first output step
  is 72 % of the source speed with the fix, and 2 % without it. The speed then falls by about 0.3 m/s per frame. No
  pipeline defect was found: the root fix reduces the difference from 2.63 to 0.72 m/s. A running goal (or a later
  goal) is expected to pass.
- **Neophyte G3: part of the error is in the source.** The Synty clip played on Neophyte through Unity Humanoid
  retargeting already puts a sole 2.9 cm below the floor at the anchor. Penetration and hips-height errors include that
  offset.
- **E1 playback.** A Generic `.anim` plays only on a Generic setup of the character. A Humanoid Animator ignores
  transform curves on human bones, so E1 samples the clip with the avatar removed.
